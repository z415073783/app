Private by design

Baby data is family data. Moonling has no servers of ours, no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking. Your records stay on your device. To keep the app stable we collect crash reports and basic usage events that are not linked to your identity — and you can turn that off.

Not linked. Not tracked.

Your records stay on your device by default. You can export or back them up whenever you want, and delete everything from inside the app. Optional iCloud sync (off by default) saves your records to your private iCloud database so your own Apple devices stay in sync — Moonling can’t read it. To fix crashes and improve the app, we use Google Firebase for crash reports and basic usage events; that data is not linked to you, not used for tracking, and never includes your records, baby names or notes. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy. The App Store privacy label says exactly that: crash and usage diagnostics, not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Questions parents ask

Still unsure? Email us — a real person reads every message.

Is Moonling really free?

Yes. Unlimited logging, today’s timeline, reminders, widgets, Live Activity, 7-day trends, export/backup and up to 2 babies are free, permanently. There is no trial that turns into a read-only app.

What does the $4.99 purchase unlock?

WHO growth percentile curves, full history charts beyond 7 days, unlimited milestones with the milestone album and “First Year” pages, all themes including Moonlight, PDF visit summaries with a WHO growth chart page, and unlimited baby profiles (for multiples or a growing family). It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — not a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no Moonling cloud account. Open the app and start logging. Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple Account, is off by default, and does not create an account with us.

Where is my data stored? Can I export it?

On your device by default. Export CSV or a complete JSON backup at any time, and delete all local data from inside the app. Because there is no server of ours, we cannot see or recover your data. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, a copy is stored in your private iCloud database and Apple’s iCloud terms apply; the developer still cannot read it.

Do you collect any data?

We never collect your baby’s records, names or notes. To keep the app stable and improve it, we use Google Firebase for crash reports and basic usage events (device model, iOS version, screens used). This data is not linked to your identity, is never used to track you across apps or websites, and is never sold or shared with advertisers. The app does not use the advertising identifier and never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can turn analytics and crash reporting off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

Does it work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. Moonling supports iPhone (iOS 18.0+), iPad in landscape, and iPhone Duo in both folded and unfolded states, including Split View.

Is Moonling a medical device?

No. Moonling is a logging tool. Reference ranges from WHO Child Growth Standards. For information only - not medical advice. Talk to your pediatrician.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple Support — visit Apple’s billing support.
