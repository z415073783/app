Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026 (added disclosure for Google Firebase analytics and crash reporting) · Applies to the Moonling iOS app and this website

The short version

  • Moonling has no accounts with us, no servers operated by us, no ads and no cross-app tracking.
  • We never collect your baby’s records. Records stay on your device by default and only leave it when you export a file or turn on optional iCloud sync.
  • To keep the app stable and improve it, we use Google Firebase for crash reports and basic usage events (device model, iOS version, screens used). This diagnostic data is not linked to your identity and is never used to track you across apps or websites — see section 5.
  • Optional iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your records and photos are stored in your private iCloud database so your own Apple devices stay in sync — we cannot read them.
  • You can turn analytics and crash reporting off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

1. Who we are

Moonling is an independent iOS app made by Lianzeng (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can contact us any time at z674636160@163.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect your baby’s records. The developer does not receive, store, sell or share the content you enter in Moonling. Specifically, the Moonling app does not collect, transmit, sell or share with us:

  • names, email addresses or phone numbers (there is no sign-up or login with us);
  • your baby’s records, photos, notes, feeding amounts, or growth measurements — these stay on your device by default; if you turn on optional iCloud sync they are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot access (see section 4);
  • location data, contacts, or health data from Apple Health.

The app does collect limited diagnostic and usage data through Google Firebase: crash and diagnostic data, a device/app-instance identifier (IDFV), and product interaction events such as which screens were opened. This data is not linked to your identity, is never used to track you across apps or websites, and never includes your records, baby names, notes, feeding amounts, growth measurements or health values. It is described in full in section 5.

We do not use any advertising, attribution or cross-app tracking SDK, and the app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or request App Tracking Transparency permission. Apart from Google Firebase (section 5) and Apple’s own system services, the app does not connect to any server of ours or of another third party. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, the app uses Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit service as described in section 4; that data stays in your own iCloud account.

3. Where your data is stored

By default, everything you enter in Moonling is stored locally on your device. The app does not upload your records to us or to any third party.

The only data that leaves your device automatically is the technical diagnostic and usage data described in section 5, which is sent to Google Firebase and contains no content from your records.

  • Exports: You can export CSV or a complete JSON backup at any time. Those files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.
  • Backups: Local backups are kept on your device. If you choose to store a backup in iCloud Drive, Files or another service, that storage is controlled by you and governed by that service’s terms.
  • Deleting data: You can delete all local data from inside the app. Deleting the app also removes its local data from your device. If you had iCloud sync turned on, a copy may still be stored in your iCloud as described in section 4.

4. Optional iCloud sync (off by default)

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. You can turn it on in the app’s Settings under “Auto-sync to Apple Account”. When it is on:

  • your records — including milestone photos — are saved to your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit service, so your own iPhone and iPad (signed in to the same Apple Account) stay in sync;
  • the data is stored in your iCloud account. Moonling cannot read, access, export or delete it, and we never receive a copy;
  • Apple’s iCloud terms of service and Apple’s Privacy Policy apply to data stored in iCloud;
  • you can turn sync off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops syncing and keeps your records on this device; a copy may remain in your iCloud, because the app does not delete data from your iCloud when you turn sync off;
  • if you delete the app while sync is on, the copy in your iCloud may still be available — reinstalling the app with the same Apple Account and turning sync on can restore your records. If you want a copy you control, export a backup first;
  • to manage or delete data that apps store in your iCloud, use your device’s iCloud settings (the exact options depend on your iOS version) or contact Apple.
In short: optional iCloud sync stores your records in your own iCloud account, not with us. It is not a Moonling account, and Moonling cannot read your records.

5. Analytics and crash reporting (Google Firebase)

To keep Moonling stable and to improve it, the app uses two services from Google Firebase, provided by Google LLC: Google Analytics for Firebase (basic usage analytics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting). These are the only third-party SDKs in the app. They are not advertising, attribution or tracking services: we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What is sent:

  • Crash and other diagnostic data — for example the crash stack trace, device model, operating-system version, app version and app state at the time of a crash. Used for App Functionality (fixing crashes) and Analytics.
  • A device/app-instance identifier — the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and an app-instance ID, used only to group events from the same installation. It is not linked to your name or identity.
  • Product interaction events — for example that onboarding was completed, that a screen such as Today or Insights was viewed, that the paywall was viewed, and that a purchase was completed. For log entries we count only the kind of event (feed, sleep, diaper, pump or activity) and nothing about what it contained.

What is never sent: your baby’s records, baby names, notes, photos, dates, feeding amounts, growth measurements, health values or any other content you enter. Moonling never transmits your records to us or to Google.

Purposes and sharing: the data is used only for App Functionality and Analytics — to fix crashes and understand which features are used. It is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking, and is not sold or shared with advertisers. Google LLC processes it on our behalf as a service provider (subprocessor); its handling is described in Firebase’s privacy and security documentation and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention: Google Analytics for Firebase keeps event data for 14 months by default (this is configured in the Firebase console), and Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days.

Your choice: analytics and crash reporting are on by default and can be turned off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → “Share usage & crash data”. When it is off, no analytics or crash data is collected or sent from your device. Turning it off does not affect any logging feature. Debug builds used by the developer do not send this data unless explicitly enabled.

In short: Google Firebase receives crash reports and basic usage events that are not linked to your identity and are never used to track you. It never receives your baby’s records, names or notes.

6. In-app purchases

Moonling offers one optional one-time purchase ($4.99) that unlocks extra features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card details, or Apple Account information. Apple provides developers with aggregated sales reports that do not identify individual customers.

Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. Refund requests are handled by Apple Support.

7. Notifications, widgets and Live Activity

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets and Live Activity display data from your device. None of these features send your data to us. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, the synced copy described in section 4 applies.

8. Children’s privacy

Moonling is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children. The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app never transmits your baby’s records, names, notes or health values to us or to Google Firebase — the diagnostics described in section 5 relate to the app and the device, not to your child. Because the developer cannot access your private iCloud database, no child data is transmitted to us.

9. Your rights and choices

We do not have an account for you, we do not hold data that identifies you, and the diagnostics we receive are not linked to your identity. There is no account to delete and no personal profile for us to access, correct or transfer. You remain in full control of your data:

  • turn analytics and crash reporting on or off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy; when off, no analytics or crash data is collected on the device;
  • turn optional iCloud sync on or off at any time in Settings;
  • export or back up your data at any time; if you plan to delete the app, export a backup first if you want a copy you control;
  • delete individual entries or all local data inside the app;
  • delete the app to remove its local data from your device;
  • manage or delete data that apps store in your iCloud from your device’s iCloud settings (options depend on your iOS version), or contact Apple.

Because the diagnostics sent to Google Firebase are not linked to your identity, we cannot look up or delete data for a specific person on request; turning the setting off stops collection from your device immediately. If you have a privacy question, contact us at z674636160@163.com.

10. Third parties

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We never receive your baby’s records. The third parties involved in the app experience are:

  • Apple — for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, operating-system services, and — only if you turn it on — iCloud storage for the optional sync described in section 4. Apple handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy; we cannot access it.
  • Google LLC (Firebase) — for crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) and basic usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), as described in section 5. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider (subprocessor). It is not sold and not shared with advertisers.

11. This website

This support and policy website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, CDNs or scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) to operate and secure the service; we do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

12. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. If the change is significant, we will note it in the app or on this page. The current version always describes the app’s actual practices.
