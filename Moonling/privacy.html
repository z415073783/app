Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026 (added disclosure for optional iCloud sync) · Applies to the Moonling iOS app and this website

The short version

  • Moonling has no accounts with us, no servers operated by us, no analytics and no ads.
  • We do not collect personal data. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.
  • Your baby’s records stay on your device by default — unless you export them, back them up yourself, or turn on optional iCloud sync.
  • Optional iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your records and photos are stored in your private iCloud database so your own Apple devices stay in sync — we cannot read them.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

1. Who we are

Moonling is an independent iOS app made by Lianzeng (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can contact us any time at z674636160@163.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The developer does not receive, store, sell or share your data. Specifically, the Moonling app does not collect, transmit, sell or share with us:

  • names, email addresses or phone numbers (there is no sign-up or login with us);
  • your baby’s records, photos, notes, or growth measurements — these stay on your device by default; if you turn on optional iCloud sync they are stored in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot access (see section 4);
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics;
  • location data, contacts, or health data from Apple Health.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The app does not require any account and does not connect to a server operated by us. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, the app uses Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit service as described in section 4; that data stays in your own iCloud account.

3. Where your data is stored

By default, everything you enter in Moonling is stored locally on your device. The app does not upload it to us or to any third party.

  • Exports: You can export CSV or a complete JSON backup at any time. Those files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.
  • Backups: Local backups are kept on your device. If you choose to store a backup in iCloud Drive, Files or another service, that storage is controlled by you and governed by that service’s terms.
  • Deleting data: You can delete all local data from inside the app. Deleting the app also removes its local data from your device. If you had iCloud sync turned on, a copy may still be stored in your iCloud as described in section 4.

4. Optional iCloud sync (off by default)

iCloud sync is optional and off by default. You can turn it on in the app’s Settings under “Auto-sync to Apple Account”. When it is on:

  • your records — including milestone photos — are saved to your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit service, so your own iPhone and iPad (signed in to the same Apple Account) stay in sync;
  • the data is stored in your iCloud account. Moonling cannot read, access, export or delete it, and we never receive a copy;
  • Apple’s iCloud terms of service and Apple’s Privacy Policy apply to data stored in iCloud;
  • you can turn sync off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops syncing and keeps your records on this device; a copy may remain in your iCloud, because the app does not delete data from your iCloud when you turn sync off;
  • if you delete the app while sync is on, the copy in your iCloud may still be available — reinstalling the app with the same Apple Account and turning sync on can restore your records. If you want a copy you control, export a backup first;
  • to manage or delete data that apps store in your iCloud, use your device’s iCloud settings (the exact options depend on your iOS version) or contact Apple.
In short: optional iCloud sync stores your records in your own iCloud account, not with us. It is not a Moonling account, and Moonling cannot read your records.

5. In-app purchases

Moonling offers one optional one-time purchase ($4.99) that unlocks extra features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment card details, or Apple Account information. Apple provides developers with aggregated sales reports that do not identify individual customers.

Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. Refund requests are handled by Apple Support.

6. Notifications, widgets and Live Activity

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets and Live Activity display data from your device. None of these features send your data to us. If you turn on optional iCloud sync, the synced copy described in section 4 applies.

7. Children’s privacy

Moonling is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children. The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the developer collects no data at all and cannot access your private iCloud database, no child data is transmitted to us.

8. Your rights and choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no account to delete and no data for us to access, correct or transfer. You remain in full control of your data:

  • turn optional iCloud sync on or off at any time in Settings;
  • export or back up your data at any time; if you plan to delete the app, export a backup first if you want a copy you control;
  • delete individual entries or all local data inside the app;
  • delete the app to remove its local data from your device;
  • manage or delete data that apps store in your iCloud from your device’s iCloud settings (options depend on your iOS version), or contact Apple.

If you have a privacy question, contact us at z674636160@163.com.

9. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect it. The only third party involved in the app experience is Apple: for App Store distribution, in-app purchases, operating-system services, and — only if you turn it on — iCloud storage for the optional sync described in section 4. Apple handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy; we cannot access it.

10. This website

This support and policy website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, CDNs or scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) to operate and secure the service; we do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

11. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. If the change is significant, we will note it in the app or on this page. The current version always describes the app’s actual practices.
