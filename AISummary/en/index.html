AISummary
AI-powered notes, summaries, and to-dos — make every record count
中文

AISummary is an all-in-one productivity app for note-taking, AI summaries, to-do lists, and insights. With iCloud sync, widgets, and a share extension, capture and review anywhere.

Key Features

📝

Organize Your Notes

Search, categorize, tag, and notebook — rich text, images, and Markdown support.

✅

Today's To-Do

Calendar view for task progress; extract to-dos from notes or AI summaries in one tap.

🤖

Smart AI Summary

Auto-summarize notes by week, month, or year with custom templates and follow-up actions.

📊

Insights at a Glance

Track writing habits and note growth with hourly statistics.

✨

AI Writing Assistant

Polish, expand, continue, translate, and extract to-dos to boost your writing flow.

📤

Export & Share

Export as Markdown, PDF, or image cards for easy sharing and archiving.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Notes, summaries, and images sync via iCloud across your devices.

🔔

Daily Review Reminder

Get reminded to revisit notes from this day in past years.

Use Cases

📔 Daily journal 💼 Work review 📚 Study notes 🗓️ Weekly/monthly recap
🔒 Privacy: Notes are stored on your device by default, with optional iCloud sync. AI features send relevant text to an AI provider for processing. See our Privacy Policy.
