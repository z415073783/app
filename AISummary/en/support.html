FAQ

  • How do I use AI Summary?

    Go to the Summary tab, choose a time range (week/month/year), and tap generate. AI Summary is a Pro feature; free users can use other AI features once per day.

  • How do I enable iCloud sync?

    Open Settings → iCloud Sync and turn on Auto Sync. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and have granted this app access to iCloud Drive.

  • How do I back up and restore data?

    In Settings → Backup & Restore, tap Back Up Now to create a backup. Tap a previous backup to restore. With iCloud sync enabled, backups are stored in iCloud.

  • How do I manage my subscription?

    Subscriptions renew monthly and can be managed or cancelled anytime in iPhone Settings → Your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
