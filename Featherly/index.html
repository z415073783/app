Does Featherly upload my images?

No. Featherly has no network entitlement and no server. Decoding, compression and export all happen on your Mac.

What is free, exactly?

Unlimited single-image compression, all supported output formats, before/after comparison, multiple sizes with 1x/2x/3x variants, saved output presets, resizing, Web/Share/Email/Thumbnail recipes, custom filename suffixes, metadata stripping, copying and exporting. Only batch export is Pro — and Pro also includes the `featherly` command-line tool.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time non-consumable App Store purchase. No renewals and no account. You can restore it with the same Apple ID.

Will my original files be changed?

Never. Featherly writes a new file to the location you choose. Your originals stay exactly as they were.

What formats are supported?

Reads common ImageIO formats including JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF and WebP. Writes JPEG, HEIC, PNG and WebP.
