Featherly

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Featherly for macOS 1.x
The short version. Featherly processes your images entirely on your Mac. It does not upload, transmit, or back up your photos. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Images and files

When you add an image, Featherly reads it from the location you choose and decodes it locally with macOS ImageIO. Compression, resizing, metadata stripping, and export all happen on your device. Originals are never modified.

The queue and previews live in memory and are discarded when you quit. Exported files are written only to the destination you select in the save or folder panel. The app does not access Photos, contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, or screen content.

Metadata

Featherly strips location, camera, and lens metadata from exported images. It does not read or share this metadata.

Preferences

Featherly stores a small set of preferences locally with Apple's UserDefaults: output format, quality, resize setting, longest-side value, and filename suffix. This data never leaves your Mac and is not linked to an identity.

Purchases

Featherly Pro is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase handled by Apple. Apple may process purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy. Featherly receives only the local StoreKit entitlement needed to unlock Pro features. No receipt server is operated by the developer.

Support email

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message solely to respond. Support correspondence is not used for marketing or tracking.

Children

Featherly does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
