What is free, and what is Pro?

Free: unlimited single-image compression, JPEG/PNG/HEIC/WebP output, before/after comparison, resizing, recipes, custom filename suffixes, metadata stripping, exporting and copying. Pro: batch export only. Pro is one non-consumable purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro screen and click “Restore Purchase”, use Settings → Restore Purchase, or choose Featherly Pro → Restore Purchases from the app menu. Use the same Apple ID that bought Pro.

Does Featherly upload my photos?

No. The app is sandboxed, has no networking code, and no server. All processing is local.

Are my originals modified?

No. Featherly creates a new file and never writes to the original image.

Why does a PNG sometimes get larger?

PNG is lossless, so the quality slider does not apply. Use Resize, or choose JPEG/HEIC/WebP for photographic images.

Which formats are supported?

Reads common ImageIO formats: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP and more. Writes JPEG, HEIC, PNG and WebP.
