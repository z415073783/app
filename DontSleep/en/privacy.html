2. What Personal Information We Collect

When you use certain features of this App, we may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Information necessary for specific features:
    • Camera images: When you use the fatigue detection feature, the App invokes the device's front camera to capture your facial images in real time. These images are used solely for real-time analysis of your eye state (open / half-closed / closed) to determine fatigue levels and provide timely reminders. The images are processed locally on your device in real time and are never saved or uploaded to any server. See Section 3 "Camera and Face Data" for full details.
  • Locally stored information:
    • Preferences: Settings you adjust in the App (such as detection sensitivity, frame rate, resolution, alert volume, power-saving mode, etc.) are stored locally on your device so your personalized configuration is preserved after restart.
    • Detection history: The App stores your historical detection records locally (such as detection duration and eye-closure statistics) to show your latest detection statistics on the welcome screen before a new session. Records are retained for 3 months and automatically cleaned up afterwards.

3. Camera and Face Data

DontSleep uses the front camera to detect the user's face and eye state in real time for the sole purpose of fatigue detection. All processing is performed entirely on the user's device using Apple's Vision framework.

  • What face data is processed: The App does not collect, record, or store any face data. While a fatigue-detection session is running, each camera video frame is processed transiently in device memory. The App temporarily reads only the normalized coordinates of the eye landmarks in the current frame to compute a single Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) value that measures how open or closed the eyes are. No facial images, video frames, or facial geometry are captured or saved.
  • Use: The result is used solely to determine the user's fatigue state in real time and to trigger local sound and visual alerts. No other use is made of face-related information.
  • Storage and retention: Nothing is stored. Each video frame and its derived eye coordinates exist only for the few milliseconds needed to process that frame and are discarded immediately afterwards. No face data remains after the user stops a detection session.
  • Sharing and disclosure: Face data is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The App has no networking functionality, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party frameworks.
  • Deletion: Because no face data is ever saved, there is nothing to delete. The only data the App persists are fatigue session statistics (session start date, total duration, and accumulated closed-eye/half-closed durations), which contain no face, image, or biometric data. These statistics are stored locally on the device, automatically removed after 90 days, and can be manually deleted at any time via the "Clear All Records" button in the Statistics screen.

You can also manually delete all detection records at any time via the "Clear All Records" button in the Statistics screen.
  • Security measures: We adopt industry-standard physical, technical and administrative security measures to protect your personal information. Since your images and detection data remain on your device and are never transmitted over the network, third parties cannot access your data remotely.
