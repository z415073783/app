Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • How do I use this app?

    Open the app and place your phone securely in front of you so the front camera can clearly capture your face. Tap "Start Detection" to begin real-time eye state monitoring. When fatigue signals are detected, the app will alert you with sound and visual cues.

  • Does the app save or upload my images?

    No. All eye state detection is performed locally on your device in real time. No images are saved or uploaded, and the app requires no network connection.

  • Why does the app need camera access?

    The app uses the front camera to analyze your eye state (open / half-closed / closed) in real time to determine fatigue levels and remind you in time. All analysis happens locally on your device.
