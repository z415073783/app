Why Choose DontSleep?

Drowsiness often strikes without warning. DontSleep monitors your eye state through the front camera and alerts you the moment you start dozing off — helping you drive, study, and work safely.

👁️

Real-Time Eye Detection

Powered by Apple Vision framework, accurately detects open, half-closed, and closed eyes with millisecond responsiveness.

🎯

Dual Fatigue Detection

Combines prolonged eye-closure duration with PERCLOS metrics to catch both sudden danger and accumulated fatigue.

🔔

Progressive Audio Alerts

Three-level alarms — Remind → Warning → Emergency — grow stronger the longer your eyes stay closed.

🔋

Smart Power-Saving Mode

One-tap switch to low frame rate and resolution to reduce heat and save battery; restores your custom settings automatically.

☀️

Auto Brightness Control

Dims the screen during detection, restores brightness when you look, and dims again when you look away.

🗣️

Hourly Voice Reminders

A gentle voice reminds you to rest every full hour of continuous detection.

📊

Statistics & History

Tracks detection duration and eye-closure ratio, keeping up to 3 months of history to review your habits.

⚙️

Fully Customizable

Adjust detection sensitivity, alert volume, frame rate, and resolution to fit any scenario.

Where to Use

🚗 Driving 📚 Studying 💻 Late-night Work
🔒 Privacy First: All detection runs entirely on-device. No network connection, no upload — your privacy stays with you.
