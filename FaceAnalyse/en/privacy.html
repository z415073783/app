Summary

Face Explorer does not require an account. The photo, birth information, name, MBTI type or tarot question you choose to provide is sent over an encrypted connection to our own server. The server computes deterministic facts in memory (e.g., Four Pillars/Five Elements, zodiac, tarot cards, life path number, synastry relations) and a third-party AI service (DeepSeek) turns them into an entertainment reading. We do not store your photo, birth information, name, question or report text on our servers, and we do not use them for advertising or model training. We use only a randomly generated device identifier for request verification, daily limits and abuse prevention. For two-person scenes, you must obtain the other person's consent. Completed readings are saved only on your device (up to the latest 50, including facts, full report and a compressed thumbnail; no original image). You can delete/clear them at any time; we do not upload local history or thumbnails.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Provided by you

  • Photo: one photo you choose from your library or take with the camera for the face/classic-face/palm reading (a palm photo for palm reading). It is used only for this reading and released after processing (see Section 2).
  • Birth information: calendar type, birth date, birth time or "time unknown", birth city/longitude/timezone, and gender you enter for Bazi/horoscope/birthday/two-person scenes. Used only in memory for this reading; not stored.
  • Name: the name you enter for name/synastry readings. Used only for this reading; not stored.
  • MBTI type: the four-letter type you select. Used only for this reading; not stored.
  • Tarot question: the optional question you enter (0–200 characters). Used only for this reading; not stored or logged.
  • Two-person information (third party): partner birth information and optional name/gender for synastry/Bazi compatibility. You must have the other person's consent. Used only for this reading; not stored.
  • Language preference: used to choose the report language (Simplified Chinese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish or Japanese).

1.2 Generated automatically

  • Device identifier (Device ID): a random UUID generated when the app is installed and stored in the system Keychain. It is not IDFA, IDFV, a serial number or a phone number and cannot identify you personally. It is used for request signing, per-device daily limits, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.
  • Request metadata: time, duration, status code, image bytes and dimensions, token usage and server error codes. Used for quotas, cost accounting and service reliability.
  • Network logs: our server keeps access logs for security and troubleshooting (time, path, status code, duration, device identifier, IP address) for a short retention period. Logs do not contain the photo, its base64 data or the report text.

1.3 Purchases

  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store / StoreKit). We do not collect or store your payment card number, Apple ID or billing information.
  • Analysis credits (1 free per day; +6 per in-app purchase) are stored locally on your device (Keychain) and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Apple provides us with limited transaction status so credits can be added to your device.

1.4 What we do NOT collect

We do not collect account credentials, phone number, email (unless you email us), contacts, precise location, other photos in your library, IDFA/advertising identifiers, Face ID or other biometric templates, or information about people you share reports with. We do not integrate advertising or third-party tracking SDKs and do not track you across apps.

Name, birth date/time/place/longitude, gender, MBTI type, tarot question and two-person information are processed only when you start the relevant reading, are not stored, not used for profiling, advertising or model training, and are not requested when you do not use those scenes.

2. How Photos Are Processed

  1. Only the photo you select: the app does not read your library in bulk. It uses the system photo picker or camera to minimize permission scope.
  2. Local compression and metadata removal: before upload, the photo is redrawn as JPEG (long edge about 1280 px, quality about 0.8), orientation is baked into the pixels, and EXIF, GPS location and camera-device metadata are removed.
  3. Encrypted transfer: the photo is base64-encoded in an HTTPS request to our own server (hk.zengliangmin.cn), protected by request signatures against tampering and replay.
  4. Forwarded to a third-party AI: our server forwards the photo to DeepSeek for visual analysis. See DeepSeek's privacy policy: Chinese / English. We forward data in a minimized form; the provider's retention, training and processing practices are governed by its own policy, and we make no commitments beyond it.
  5. In-memory processing, no persistence: our server validates, forwards and releases the photo in memory only. We do not write photos to server disks, do not use object storage, do not log them and do not back them up. Memory is released when analysis ends or the request is closed. Report text is streamed to your device and is not stored on our servers.
  6. Photos are not stored long-term on the device: the app keeps the photo in memory only for the current session; it is not written to your library or app cache. Local history stores result facts, the full report and one compressed thumbnail (240–320px long edge JPEG, EXIF removed, not the original); deleting history or clearing all removes thumbnails. Photos taken with the camera are processed the same way as library photos: used only for this analysis, the original is not stored on our servers; local history may keep a compressed thumbnail, which you can delete at any time.
  7. No training or advertising: we do not use your photos to train AI models, for advertising, for sale, or for sharing with research institutions.
  8. Report sharing: the share card/text is generated on-device only when you tap Share. It contains only the displayed report excerpt, scene, date, brand and disclaimer; it does not include birth details, names, questions, photos, two-person information or raw input. It is not uploaded or posted automatically; the destination third-party app handles it under its own privacy policy.

4. Retention of Device ID and Usage Data

  • Device ID: stored in your device Keychain for request signing and daily limits; on our usage table, per-device detail is deleted after 30 days.
  • Aggregated usage without identifiers (total requests, tokens, image bytes): retained for up to 365 days for cost and capacity planning.
  • Server access logs: retained short-term under log rotation (typically no more than 30 days) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Local credit ledger: stored in your device Keychain until you uninstall the app or clear its data; after uninstall, Keychain items may remain so unused credits can be restored, depending on system behavior.
  • Birth information/name/MBTI/tarot question/two-person information/photos/reports: not written to server disks, not logged; released after the request ends.
  • Local history/thumbnails: stored only in your device's Application Support, up to 50 items; oldest removed beyond 50; deleted on individual delete/clear all; excluded from iCloud backup; never uploaded. Viewing/deleting history does not change credits or purchases.

5. Data Security

  • HTTPS is used end to end; APIs use HMAC request signatures, timestamps and nonces to prevent tampering and replay.
  • The AI provider's real API key exists only on our server; the client holds no cloud-provider credentials.
  • We follow data minimization: no photos on disk, no report text in logs, and logs contain metadata only.
  • No internet transmission or processing can be absolutely secure. Please avoid uploading photos containing sensitive information (e.g., identity documents, addresses, or other people's private information).

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Do not use analysis: you may browse the app without selecting a photo; no photo is uploaded unless you start an analysis.
  • Deny permissions: you may deny photo-library or camera access; the app will explain but will not force you. You can still view the privacy policy and purchase credits.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings. Since purchased credits are tracked locally, you may need "Restore Purchases" (based on transaction history, subject to the limitations in the Terms of Use).
  • Contact us: email z674636160@163.com to request access, correction, deletion or to file a complaint. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for your device identifier or request time to locate records; we will respond within the period required by law.
  • Withdraw consent: you may stop using the service. Completed processing cannot be undone, but this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

7. Minors

The app is rated 12+ and is not designed for children under 12. If you are under 18, please use it with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. We do not actively collect age or date of birth. If a guardian believes a minor uploaded a photo without consent, contact us and we will help handle the relevant records.

12. Local History, Thumbnails and Completion Notifications (New in v2.1)

  1. Local history content: successful readings are saved on the device with scene, completion time, language, facts, full report and one compressed thumbnail (240–320px long edge JPEG, EXIF removed for image scenes; scene icon placeholder for text scenes). We do not store the original image, photo base64, raw input objects (birth date, name, tarot question, two-person raw input); derived display facts (e.g., solar/lunar date, Four Pillars, Five Elements) may be stored locally with the result; names/questions naturally appearing in the report are stored locally only as part of that report.
  2. Device-only, not uploaded: history and thumbnails live in the app's Application Support, are not sent to our server or third-party AI, are not synced across devices, and are excluded from iCloud backup.
  3. Limit and deletion: keep at most the latest 50 items; adding the 51st removes the oldest; individual delete and clear-all are supported with confirmation and are irreversible; deleting history cancels its pending local notification.
  4. Notification permission and purpose: before the first reading we ask for notification permission to remind you when a reading completes. Declining does not block analysis and only in-app banners are used. Notifications contain only the scene name and a generic "tap to view" message — no birth information, name, question, report text or thumbnail; you can disable them in system settings.
  5. Billing separation: saving/viewing/deleting history does not refund, recharge or consume credits; "read again" starts a new reading and is charged as a new reading.
  6. Birth information/name/MBTI/tarot question/two-person information: still processed in server memory under this policy, not written to disk/logs and no server-side history; local history does not additionally store raw inputs.
  7. Age: local history/notifications are also 12+ entertainment; not used for advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking.
