Face Explorer Support

Thank you for using Face Explorer. No account is required: pick a photo, and our self-hosted service forwards it to a third-party AI model, which streams back a facial-feature report.

FAQ

  • Analysis failed / no face detected? Try a clear, front-facing, well-lit photo without occlusion, and check your network connection.
  • Are my photos stored? No. Photos are processed only in memory for this analysis; our servers do not write them to disk or log their contents. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do credits work? Each device gets 1 free analysis per day. In-app purchases are consumables (+6 analyses per purchase). Credits are deducted only when an analysis completes successfully and is analyzable; failures, cancellations and no-face results do not consume credits.
  • Purchased credits missing / new device? Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings. Consumables are restored from Apple's transaction history; switching devices or clearing data may not fully restore them. Refunds and billing are handled by Apple.
  • How do I delete local data? Delete the app or clear its data in system settings; this also clears the local credit ledger.
  • Is the report accurate? Reports are AI-generated for entertainment and general skincare reference only, not medical or professional beauty advice.
