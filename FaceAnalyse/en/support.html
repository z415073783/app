Face Explorer Support

Thank you for using Face Explorer. No account is required. Choose a reading, provide a photo or birth details, and our self-hosted service returns an AI-generated entertainment reading.

FAQ

  • The analysis failed / no face detected? Try a clear, front-facing, well-lit photo without occlusion, and check your network connection.
  • Are my photos or birth details stored on your servers? No. Photos, birth details, names, tarot questions and partner information are processed only in memory for that reading. Our servers do not write them to disk or log their contents. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Where are my results saved? Successful readings are saved locally on your device (last 50, including a small thumbnail) so you can view them anytime under History. They are not uploaded. You can delete one entry or clear all history in the app.
  • How do credits work? Each device gets 1 free reading per day. In-app purchases are consumables (+6 readings per purchase). Credits are deducted only when a reading completes successfully and is analyzable; failures, cancellations and no-face/content-filtered results do not consume credits. Viewing or deleting history never costs credits.
  • Can I run more than one reading at a time? Yes. Up to two readings run in parallel on a device; additional readings wait in a queue and start automatically. Leaving the page does not cancel a running reading.
  • Why didn't I get a completion notification? Notifications require permission. If permission is denied, the app still shows an in-app banner when a reading finishes. You can manage notifications in Settings.
  • Purchased credits missing / new device? Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings. Consumables are restored from Apple's transaction history; switching devices or clearing data may not fully restore them. Refunds and billing are handled by Apple.
  • How do I delete local data? Delete the app or clear its data in system settings; this also clears local history and the local credit ledger.
  • Is the report accurate? Readings are AI-generated for entertainment and general lifestyle reference only, not medical, psychological, legal, financial or relationship advice.
